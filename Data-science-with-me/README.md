Project Architecture

 Data Preprocessing & EDA
Dataset: [Ames Housing](https://www.kaggle.com/c/house-prices-advanced-regression-techniques)
Business Context: Real estate pricing relies on dozens of interdependent variables. This phase focuses on cleaning a high-dimensional dataset to ensure reliable model inputs.
Techniques: Median/Mode Imputation, Interquartile Range (IQR) Outlier Filtering, Correlation Heatmaps.


 Feature Engineering
Dataset: [Customer Segmentation](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/fahmidachowdhury/customer-segmentation-data-for-marketing-analysis)
Business Context: Raw transactional data is often not immediately predictive. This phase transforms sales records into actionable customer behavior metrics.
Techniques: RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Creation, Standard Scaling, One-Hot Encoding.

 Model Selection & Training
Dataset: [Telco Customer Churn](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/blastchar/telco-customer-churn)
Business Context: Retaining an existing customer is cheaper than acquiring a new one. This phase builds predictive models to flag at-risk subscriptions.
Techniques: Train/Test Splitting, Logistic Regression (Baseline), Random Forest Classification (Ensemble).

 Model Evaluation
Dataset:[Credit Card Fraud Detection](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mlg-ulb/creditcardfraud)
Business Context: Fraudulent transactions account for a fraction of a percent of total volume. This phase tackles the mathematical challenge of extreme class imbalance.
Techniques: SMOTE Oversampling, F1-Score Optimization, Precision-Recall Curves, Confusion Matrices.
